Substat Quality (100% is max substats)

Score system is based on the following

Total score = product of all other scores
DPS score = product of all DPS sources :{(100%+dps)/100%}
Skill score = product of all skill sources :{(100%+skill)/100%}
Survival score = 5(100%+Regen) or DPS * (Lifesteal) divided by 2(range) or 5(melee)
Survival is scaled to (100%+Health) and (100%-block)
RNG = product of all RNG sources :{(100%-rng)/10%}
scores are based on 100% Substats
